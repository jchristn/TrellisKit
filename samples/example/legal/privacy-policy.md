# privacy policy

Sample privacy policy for the Example Community property. Replace with your own.

If this community enables optional product analytics, it collects counts of signup steps, profile completion, the first contribution, and notification opens. Event payloads contain declared categories rather than message text, email addresses, or other free text. Hourly aggregates contain no member identifiers. Minimal account-linked milestone records prevent duplicate counts and are removed when an account closes. Property-defined events use only explicitly declared category values. Product event collection is off by default; describe any enabled collection and your retention policy before using this template.
