# participation agreement

Sample participation agreement for the Example Community property. Replace with your own.

**Version:** 1.0

## Summary

- Activities discussed in the community carry their own risks.
- You take part at your own risk and follow the community's guidelines.
- You must accept this agreement separately when you sign up, and again when it changes.

## 1. Acceptance

You accept this agreement by checking its box when you create an account.

## 2. Your responsibility

You are responsible for how you use what you learn here.
